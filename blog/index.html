Why PP Corrugated? The Case for Polypropylene Packaging

The shift from single-use cardboard and waxed-fiber boxes to reusable polypropylene corrugated packaging is one of the clearest ROI improvements available to any operation that moves product regularly. Here is what drives that ROI:

100% Waterproof Construction

Traditional cardboard — including waxed produce boxes — degrades the moment it contacts moisture, compressed ice, or high-humidity cold-storage environments. Waxed cardboard cannot be recycled and is increasingly restricted in some municipalities. PP corrugated is inherently waterproof through its entire cross-section. It can be pressure-washed, sanitized with food-grade cleaners, and returned to service immediately after cleaning. In wet environments like seafood processing, fresh produce cold chains, and poultry facilities, this is not a minor advantage — it is operationally transformative.

Reuse Ratio: 50 Trips vs. 1

Industry-standard PP corrugated containers from a quality manufacturer like Elipacko achieve 50+ reuse cycles under normal operating conditions. Compare that to single-use cardboard: the math is straightforward. If your operation ships 10,000 units per month, the difference between 50 reuses and 1 is the difference between purchasing 120,000 boxes per year versus 2,400. The payback period is typically under 12 months even accounting for the higher upfront cost of PP corrugated. Contact Elipacko directly for factory-direct pricing on minimum order quantities that make sense for your volume.

Forklift-Ready and Racking-Compatible

PP corrugated Gaylord boxes, pallet bins, and stackable containers are engineered to interface directly with standard forklift tines and pallet racking systems. Unlike cardboard, which loses structural integrity when wet or heavily loaded, high-quality PP corrugated maintains its rated static and dynamic load capacity across the full range of operating conditions. This means no unexpected collapses in cold storage, no torn corners on forklifts, and no product damage events traced back to packaging failure.

FDA Food-Grade Options

Elipacko manufactures PP Corrugated Boxes using polypropylene resin compliant with FDA 21 CFR 177.1520, making the material suitable for direct food contact applications. For USDA-inspected meat plants, HACCP-certified poultry facilities, and FDA-regulated produce operations, using compliant packaging is not optional — it's required. PP corrugated also supports HACCP color-coding programs, allowing facilities to designate specific container colors for different product zones and prevent cross-contamination.

Anti-Dumping Duty: 0% on Thailand-Manufactured Product

Thailand-manufactured PP corrugated enters the US at 0% anti-dumping duty. Elipacko also manufactures in Vietnam and China, giving flexibility to source from the most favorable origin based on your supply chain requirements. This trade policy advantage directly reduces landed cost compared to suppliers from duty-affected origins, and it's a factor that experienced procurement professionals increasingly weigh when evaluating supplier proposals.

Why PP Corrugated Has Taken Over

The packaging industry has been quietly undergoing a material revolution. Waxed cardboard — the standard for cold and wet environments for decades — fails at moisture, collapses under repeated loading, and is a single-use cost that adds up fast. PP corrugated flips every one of those weaknesses.

  • 100% waterproof: No absorption, no structural degradation in wet or cold environments. Hoseable and reusable after contact with liquid, blood, ice, or condensation.
  • 50:1 reuse ratio vs cardboard: A quality PP corrugated boxes from a reputable manufacturer will complete 50 or more full load cycles before retirement. Cardboard is one and done.
  • Forklift-ready: Proper PP corrugated is engineered for pallet and forklift operations, not just hand carries. Stack heights, compression loads, and lip clearances are all designed for real warehouse environments.
  • FDA food-grade options: The right resin formulation and clean manufacturing environment means PP corrugated is certified food-safe for direct contact with produce, poultry, seafood, and processed foods.
  • No ISPM-15 compliance issues: Unlike wood packaging, PP corrugated requires no heat treatment for international shipment and carries no phytosanitary risk.
  • Zero anti-dumping duty from Thailand: Thailand-manufactured PP corrugated currently enters the US at 0% ADD, making properly sourced product price-competitive despite higher quality. Elipacko manufactures in Thailand, Vietnam, and China — with US production in development.
