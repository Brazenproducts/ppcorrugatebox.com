Why PP Corrugated Has Taken Over

The packaging industry has been quietly undergoing a material revolution. Waxed cardboard — the standard for cold and wet environments for decades — fails at moisture, collapses under repeated loading, and is a single-use cost that adds up fast. PP corrugated flips every one of those weaknesses.

  • 100% waterproof: No absorption, no structural degradation in wet or cold environments. Hoseable and reusable after contact with liquid, blood, ice, or condensation.
  • 50:1 reuse ratio vs cardboard: A quality PP corrugated boxes from a reputable manufacturer will complete 50 or more full load cycles before retirement. Cardboard is one and done.
  • Forklift-ready: Proper PP corrugated is engineered for pallet and forklift operations, not just hand carries. Stack heights, compression loads, and lip clearances are all designed for real warehouse environments.
  • FDA food-grade options: The right resin formulation and clean manufacturing environment means PP corrugated is certified food-safe for direct contact with produce, poultry, seafood, and processed foods.
  • No ISPM-15 compliance issues: Unlike wood packaging, PP corrugated requires no heat treatment for international shipment and carries no phytosanitary risk.
  • Zero anti-dumping duty from Thailand: Thailand-manufactured PP corrugated currently enters the US at 0% ADD, making properly sourced product price-competitive despite higher quality. Elipacko manufactures in Thailand, Vietnam, and China — with US production in development.
